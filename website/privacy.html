Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Overview

Monknot is a local-first macOS workspace. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, cloud storage, payment processing, or AI services. The app is designed to work with files you select on your Mac.

Data Monknot Processes

To provide its features, Monknot processes the files and folders you choose to open, including document contents and metadata such as names and paths. It also processes searches, edits, previews, PDF annotations and exports, and terminal input and output while you use those features.

Monknot stores local preferences and application state such as your selected appearance, theme settings, recent workspaces and documents, open tabs, layout settings, export settings, and a security-scoped bookmark for a workspace you allow the app to reopen.

Files and Local Data

Your workspace files remain on your Mac or in the storage location you chose. Monknot reads and writes those files only as needed for actions you request, such as editing, replacing text, annotating a PDF, or exporting a document.

Preferences, workspace access information, recent-item history, and other application state are stored locally using macOS storage. Search indexes and similar working data are maintained locally and are not uploaded by Monknot.

Network Requests and Third-Party Services

Monknot does not contain an external API client, perform automatic update checks, or include code that sends workspace files to Monknot. The Mac App Store build is sandboxed and does not request the macOS network-client entitlement.

HTML documents you choose to preview can contain links or references to remote resources. The Mac App Store build does not enable outbound network access. In another build with network permission, WebKit may request a resource named by the document; Monknot does not add those references.

Commands you choose to run in the embedded terminal may interact with services you direct them to, subject to the build’s macOS permissions. Those commands and services are under your control and are governed by the relevant service’s terms and privacy policy.

The Monknot website is hosted by Vercel and links to GitHub for downloads. When you visit the website or follow a download link, those providers may process ordinary request information under their own privacy policies. Monknot does not add analytics, tracking pixels, or non-essential cookies to the website.

Analytics and Crash Reports

Monknot does not include analytics, telemetry, tracking, or a third-party crash-reporting service. The app does not automatically send usage data or crash reports to Monknot.

Support Communications

If you email support, the email address, message, and any technical details or attachments you choose to provide are processed to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue. Email delivery and storage are also handled by the email providers used by you and Monknot. Please include only information needed for support.

Data Retention

Files in your workspace remain under your control. Local preferences and application state remain on your Mac until you change them or remove the related app data. Monknot does not keep a server-side copy of that local data.

Support emails may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, troubleshoot, maintain support history, and meet legal or security obligations. No fixed support-email retention period is currently specified.

Children’s Privacy

Monknot is not directed to children and does not include accounts, advertising, analytics, or features designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information through a support email, contact us so it can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Monknot’s features or data practices change. The current version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@monknot.app.
